# Rising Lions — Content Strategy

## Brand Voice
**Tone:** Authoritative, optimistic, pragmatic, historically aware.
**Angle:** We're not utopians — we're traders. Commerce built the Silk Road. It can rebuild bridges now.
**Audiences:** Iranian businesses (Farsi + English), Iranian diaspora (English), Israeli/Jewish partners (English + Hebrew context).

### Publishing Schedule
- **7 days/week** during launch phase, settling to **5 days/week** post-launch
- Published at **06:00 UTC** (catches Tehran morning, London morning, East Coast US evening)

### Content Pillars

| Pillar | Frequency | Description |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| 🏛️ **Sanctions & Compliance** | 2×/week | Legal updates, licensing changes, what's now allowed |
| 📈 **Market Intelligence** | 2×/week | Sector reports, pricing, trade data, opportunity maps |

| 🤝 **Community Spotlight** | 1×/week | Member success story or interview |
| 🌍 **Geopolitical Context** | 1×/week | Analysis relevant to trade relationships |

### Article Format Standards
- **Length:** 800–1,400 words for news/intel; 1,800–3,000 for guides
- **Structure:** Hook → Context → Key Facts → What It Means for Traders → CTA
- **Always include:** A sidebar CTA to join the waitlist or download the whitepaper
- **SEO targets:** "trading with Iran," "Iran import export," "Iran business opportunities," "Iran diaspora investment"

## 2. LinkedIn Content Calendar

### Account Setup
- **Company Page:** Rising Lions (therisinglions.com)
- **Personal Page:** Founder/CEO — the face of the movement
- **Posting frequency:** 5×/week company + 3×/week founder personal
- **Target:** 10,000 followers in first 6 months

### LinkedIn Content Mix

| Format | Frequency | Purpose |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| 📊 Insight carousel (10 slides) | 2×/week | Highest reach, save-worthy data |
| 📝 Long-form article | 1×/week | Authority building, SEO |
| 🎥 Short video (60–90 sec) | 1×/week | Engagement, humanise brand |
| 💬 Opinion/hot take post | 1×/week | Conversation starter |
| 📣 Community highlight | 1×/week | Social proof |
| 🔁 Curated share + commentary | 2×/week | Topical relevance |

### Weekly LinkedIn Calendar

**Monday — Insight Carousel**
Data-rich, shareable, save-worthy. e.g.:
*"Iran's economy by the numbers: 10 stats that will change how you think about this market"*

**Tuesday — Opinion Post**
Short, punchy take on a current event. e.g.:
*"Hot take: The biggest mistake Western businesses make about Iran is not doing business with Iran."*

**Wednesday — Long-Form Article**
Repurpose best site article. Aim for 1,200–1,800 words on LinkedIn.
e.g.: *"I Spent 3 Months Studying How to Trade with Iran Legally. Here's What I Found."*

**Thursday — Short Video**
30–90 seconds. Talking head or animated data. e.g.:
*"One chart that explains why the Iranian market is the biggest missed opportunity of the decade."*

**Friday — Community/Member Highlight**
Quote card + story. Celebrates members, builds social proof.
e.g.: *"From hesitant diaspora investor to closing a $500K deal. Meet Arash."*
