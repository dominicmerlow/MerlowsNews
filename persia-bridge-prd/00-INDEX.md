## Mission Statement

Persia Bridge is the digital community and commerce platform of The Cyrus Accord — an international business network connecting Iranian and Jewish/Israeli diaspora communities to rebuild and invest in a post-regime Iran. The platform provides the infrastructure for relationship-building, commerce, deal-making, knowledge exchange, and cultural preservation that underpins The Accord's strategic mission.

---

## Design Principles

1. **Trust by Design** — Every feature is built around verified identity and community vouching. Anonymous participation is limited; credentialed participation is rewarded.
2. **Layered Access** — Content, tools, and deals are progressively unlocked as members deepen their commitment and verification.
3. **Community Before Commerce** — The Bazaar and Deal Room sit inside a community, not the other way around. Relationships drive transactions.
4. **Heritage as Foundation** — The Heritage Layer is not decorative. Persian and Jewish/Israeli cultural history is woven into the platform's identity and UX.
5. **Private by Default** — Sensitive deal flow, member contact details, and syndicate activity are never public. Privacy architecture is non-negotiable.
6. **Modular Build** — MVP modules are standalone but designed to compose. Nothing built in MVP is thrown away in the full build.
